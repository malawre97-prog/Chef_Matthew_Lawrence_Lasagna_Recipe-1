Easy Homemade Lasagna

Ingredients:

Ingredient Amount Notes
Lasagna noodles 12 noodles Cook according to package directions
Ground beef 1 lb cooked and drained
Tomato pasta sauce 24 oz Your favourite sauce
Ricotta cheese 15 oz For the cheese layer
Mozzarella cheese 2 cups Shredded
Parmesan cheese 1/2 cup Grated
Egg 1 Mixed with ricotta
Onion 1 Chopped
Peppers 2 Cubed small pices
Spinach 1/2 bag Add into sauce
Mushrooms (6-8) Optional
Celery sticks (1-3) Optional
Garlic 2 cloves Minced
Italian seasoning 1 teaspoon Optional
Black pepper 1/4 teaspoon Adjust to taste
Salt 1/2 teaspoon Adjust to taste

Instructions:

  1. Preheat the oven to 375*F (190*C)
  2. Cook the lasagna noodles according to the package directions.
  3. Brown the ground beef in a pan and drain the excess fat.
  4. Add the tomato pasta sauce and simmer for a few minutes.
  5. Mix the ricotta cheese with the egg, parmesian cheese, garlic, and italian seasoning.
  6. Layer the meat sauce, noodles, ricotta mixture, and mozzarella cheese in a baking dish.
  7. Repeat the layers until all ingredients are used.
  8. Cover with remaining mozzarella and parmesan cheese.
  9. Bake for approximately 35-40 minutes.
  10. Let the lasagna cool for 10 minutes before serving.

Tips:

  • Make sure to either oil or butter your baking pan: In order for your noodles to not stick after baking.

  • Start and end with sauce: Spread a thin layer of meat or red sauce on the bottom of your baking pan first to keep the bottom noodles from burning or sticking.

  • Press down the layers: Press each noodle layer down firmly to prevent air pockets and keep your lasagna level.

  • Cover then uncover: Bake the lasagna covered with aluminum foil or parchement paper for the first 30 minutes to trap moisture, then uncover it for the final 15 minutes to brown the cheese on top.

  • Let it rest: Allow the baked lasagna to cool and set for 15 to 20 minutes out of the oven before slicing so the layers hold their shape.
